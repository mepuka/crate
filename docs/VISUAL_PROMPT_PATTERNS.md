## KEXP Cultural Vocabulary for Visual Prompts

When writing custom prompts, use this vocabulary to ensure cultural alignment:

### KEXP-Aligned Terms
- "Independent music culture"
- "Community radio aesthetic"
- "Discovery moment"
- "Crate digging philosophy"
- "Pacific Northwest roots"
- "Earnest, not commercial"
- "DIY ethic"
- "Record store culture"
- "Basement venue vibe"
- "Listener-powered"
- "Music matters"

### Style References (KEXP-Approved)
- SubPop 90s (Seattle grunge/indie)
- KCRW tastemaker prose
- BBC 6 Music artist-as-curator
- College radio intimacy
- Independent record labels (4AD, Matador, Merge, Kill Rock Stars, K Records)
- DIY printmaking (Hatch Show Print, Aesthetic Apparatus)
- Mid-century modern jazz design (Blue Note, Impulse!)
- Analog photography (film grain, vintage color response)

### Anti-Patterns (Avoid These)
- "Professional quality" (implies commercial)
- "High-end production" (major label language)
- "Billboard-ready" (commercial chart focus)
- "Mainstream appeal" (algorithm thinking)
- "Glossy finish" / "Polished look" (corporate aesthetic)
- "Trending style" / "Instagram-ready" (social media optimization)
