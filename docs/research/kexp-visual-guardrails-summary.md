# KEXP Visual Guardrails - Quick Reference

**Date:** 2025-12-18
**For:** Quick reference when designing generative art for Crate

---

## The 10 Commandments of KEXP-Aligned Visual Design

1. **Album art is sacred** — Never obscure, distort, or replace it
2. **Warm orange is home** — Use KEXP's signature #F58216 as brand anchor
3. **Equal respect for all artists** — No tiered visual treatments (even playing field)
4. **Documentary over marketing** — Authentic, candid, contextual (not glossy/commercial)
5. **Music-first hierarchy** — Artist name > Track title > Metadata
6. **Derive from context** — Extract colors from album art, adapt to genre/era/mood
7. **Layer progressively** — Tease, don't overwhelm (progressive disclosure)
8. **Embrace imperfection** — Subtle grain, natural light, human warmth
9. **Accessibility matters** — WCAG 2.2 compliance is non-negotiable
10. **Would KEXP do this?** — If it feels like an ad or AI slop, revise

## Visual Identity at a Glance

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Radio Orange (#F58216)
- **Backgrounds:** Warm blacks (not pure #000)
- **Secondaries:** Derived from album art + genre context
- **Avoid:** Purple gradients, cold blues, neon without context

### Typography
- **Body:** IBM Plex Sans (current ✓)
- **Display:** Bricolage Grotesque or Playfair Display (replace Space Grotesk)
- **Weight:** 200 vs 700 contrasts
- **Avoid:** Inter, Roboto, Space Grotesk (generic AI defaults)

### Photography Style
- **Documentary:** Intimate, eye-focused, behind-the-scenes
- **Contextual:** Props/backgrounds that tell stories
- **Authentic:** Natural light, candid moments (not staged/glossy)
- **Avoid:** Stock photo aesthetics, marketing portraits

### Animation
- **Orchestrated:** Entrance sequences, show transitions
- **Music-reactive:** Pulse to beat, glow on transitions
- **Accessibility:** Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- **Avoid:** Constant scattered micro-interactions

## The "Would KEXP Do This?" Test

Before approving any visual:

1. ✅ Would this make an emerging artist feel respected?
2. ✅ Does this serve discovery, not engagement hacking?
3. ✅ Is this authentic, or does it feel like an ad?
4. ✅ Would a KEXP DJ approve of this?
5. ✅ Does this honor the album art, or compete with it?

If **any answer is no**, revise.

## Guardrails for Generative Art

### DO:
✅ Use album art colors as palette input
✅ Create unique signatures per artist
✅ Add subtle grain/texture (avoid clinical perfection)
✅ Frame album art respectfully (glassmorphism ✓)
✅ Support progressive disclosure (details on interaction)
✅ Adapt to genre/era/mood
✅ Maintain human oversight (AI generates, humans approve)
✅ Be transparent about AI use

### AVOID:
❌ Generic templates (one-size-fits-all)
❌ Obscuring album art
❌ Marketing aesthetics (glossy, aspirational)
❌ Tiered treatments (big names get more love)
❌ Cold color palettes
❌ Overly decorative fonts
❌ Static, non-adaptive visuals
